Our PI, Shu-Han Hsu, attended the **21st International Stirling Engine Conference (ISEC 2026)**
held in **Pau, France, August 25–27, 2026**, and gave an oral presentation in the
Thermoacoustic Devices session on August 25.

The talk, **"Operator-Based Analysis of a Loudspeaker-Coupled Thermoacoustic Stirling Engine"**
(Shu-Han Hsu and Chuan-Heng Lai), presented a physics-based operator framework that links
experimental observations with spatially distributed thermoacoustic dynamics: the linearized Rott
thermoacoustic equations are combined with a calibrated electroacoustic model of the loudspeaker
into a state-space operator whose eigenvalues directly determine the oscillation frequency and
linear growth rate, while a reduced nonlinear representation with a small set of physically
interpretable parameters captures the saturation behavior beyond the onset threshold.
