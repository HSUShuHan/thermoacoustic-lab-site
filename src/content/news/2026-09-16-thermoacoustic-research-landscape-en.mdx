The closing section of the "[Thermoacoustic Effects](/en/research/thermoacoustic-effect/)" research
introduction used to list only a handful of links. We have expanded it into a verified, curated
list of **24 active academic groups across 13 countries** and **6 thermoacoustic technology
companies**, organized by country.

Each entry carries:

- a one-line description of the group's research focus (for example, "porous-media acoustics
  (JCAL equivalent-fluid models) for thermoacoustic regenerators and stacks");
- a link to the official web page, plus a publication-record link (Google Scholar or ORCID,
  included only where the record faithfully reflects the person's output);
- for companies, a note on the technical lineage (for example, Sound Energy builds on de Blok's
  multi-stage traveling-wave architecture, and BlueHeart Energy on the ECN/TNO research line of
  Tijani and colleagues).

The inclusion criteria are simple: currently active, with an official web page, and with
thermoacoustics as a primary research line. The list will be maintained over time; corrections
and additions are welcome by <a href="mailto:bookhsu@ntut.edu.tw">email</a>.
