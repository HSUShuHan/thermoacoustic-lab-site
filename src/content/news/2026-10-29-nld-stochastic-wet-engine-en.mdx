The paper **"Stochastic identification of limit-cycle oscillations in a steam-injected wet standing-wave thermoacoustic heat engine"** by alumnus Wei-Ting Lin and PI Shu-Han Hsu is now published in *Nonlinear Dynamics* 114, 1260 (2026). It uses Kramers–Moyal stochastic identification to extract the limit-cycle amplitude, envelope restoring parameter and effective potential well of a steam-injected wet standing-wave thermoacoustic engine directly from noisy pressure records. Full text and supplementary material (raw data and code): DOI [10.1007/s11071-026-13126-y](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11071-026-13126-y).
