Our PI, Shu-Han Hsu, attended the
[**Twenty-third International Conference on Flow Dynamics (ICFD2026)**](https://www.ifs.tohoku.ac.jp/icfd/2026/),
organized by the Institute of Fluid Science, Tohoku University, and held at the
**Sendai International Center, Sendai, Japan, November 9–12, 2026**. He gave an oral presentation
in session OS22, Thermoacoustics Fundamentals and Applications, on November 11.

The talk, **"Envelope-Based Stochastic Identification of a Steam-Injected Wet Thermoacoustic Engine"**
(Wei-Ting Lin and Shu-Han Hsu), addressed a wet thermoacoustic engine driven by active steam injection.
Steam injection sustains kilopascal-scale oscillations near the boiling point, but it also drives the
acoustic field with strong broadband noise. We treat the band-passed pressure envelope as the slow
variable of a noise-forced Van der Pol–Landau oscillator and apply Kramers–Moyal estimation to recover
the limit-cycle amplitude, growth-rate parameter, effective diffusion level, amplitude distribution and
effective potential well directly from a single pressure record. The well-depth ratio, well depth over
noise strength, ranks operating points by robustness where amplitude alone cannot tell them apart.
Results cover 71 wet records at three steam rates and several heater powers, against a dry reference.
The full study is published in Nonlinear Dynamics 114, 1260 (2026), DOI
[10.1007/s11071-026-13126-y](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11071-026-13126-y).
